Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you place a bid through the Marymount Ball Auction system, we collect your name, mobile number, and bid amount. Email address and table number are optional.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to process your bid, send WhatsApp confirmation and outbid notifications (with your consent), and to contact the winning bidder for payment and collection after the auction closes.

Sharing and security

Your information is not sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for marketing purposes. WhatsApp messages are sent via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's own privacy policy governs how message delivery data is handled on their side.

Data removal

To have your data removed from our records, contact marymounthospiceauction@gmail.com.
